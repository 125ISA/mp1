Why Hawaii?

Weather

In the Oʻahu and Honolulu area, daytime temperatures in late November are usually around 27–28°C (81–82°F), while nighttime temperatures are typically around 21–23°C (70–73°F). The weather is generally very comfortable. Evenings are especially pleasant for going out to dinner, walking around, or spending time outdoors.

Experience

We can go snorkeling, hiking, take a sunset cruise, go horseback riding, visit the North Shore, go shopping, and try local food.

Food

Hawaii has many delicious local foods, such as poke, garlic shrimp, spam musubi, loco moco, and shave ice. We can also try fresh tropical fruits and local desserts like malasadas. It will be a great way to experience Hawaiian culture through food.
